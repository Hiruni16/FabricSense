Information Categories Explained

Fiber Composition Analysis

Understanding fiber content is crucial for predicting how a fabric will perform. Our database provides:

  • Natural Fibers: Cotton, silk, wool, linen - breathable, comfortable, sustainable
  • Synthetic Fibers: Polyester, nylon, acrylic - durable, easy-care, cost-effective
  • Blended Fabrics: Combining benefits of multiple fibers (e.g., 65/35 poly-cotton)
  • Performance Fibers: Spandex, Lycra - stretch and recovery properties
  • Specialty Fibers: Modal, Tencel, bamboo - sustainable alternatives

Physical & Performance Properties

Each fabric is rated across multiple performance dimensions:

  • GSM (Grams per Square Meter): Fabric weight indicating thickness and warmth
  • Thread Count: Density of weave affecting smoothness and durability
  • Breathability: Air permeability for comfort in different climates
  • Moisture Management: Absorption and wicking properties
  • Stretch & Recovery: Elasticity and return-to-shape capability
  • Pilling Resistance: How well fabric resists surface fuzzing
  • Colorfastness: Resistance to fading from washing and sunlight
