Estimation Formula & Methodology

Basic Consumption Formula

Fabric Required = (Pattern Length × Pieces) / Fabric Width + Wastage %

Our AI model uses advanced regression analysis that goes beyond basic formulas by learning from thousands of real production patterns to account for:

  • Optimal marker efficiency based on garment pieces
  • Fabric-specific shrinkage and stretch factors
  • Pattern matching requirements for prints and stripes
  • Grain line considerations and directional fabrics
  • Size grading efficiency across multiple sizes

Tips for Best Results

Getting Accurate Estimates

  • Provide accurate measurements: Double-check garment dimensions and size specifications
  • Consider design details: Include all pleats, gathers, flares, and special features
  • Know your fabric width: Always measure fabric width accurately before estimation
  • Account for pattern matching: Add extra allowance for striped or printed fabrics
  • Include lining and interfacing: Calculate separate requirements for support materials
  • Test with sample: For critical projects, create a test garment to verify consumption
  • Buy slightly extra: Purchase 10-15cm extra as safety margin for cutting errors
