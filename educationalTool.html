Transforming Fashion & Textile Education

FabricSense is more than just a fabric identification tool—it's a comprehensive educational platform designed to revolutionize how fashion and textile students learn about fabrics, consumption planning, and sustainable design practices. By combining cutting-edge AI technology with pedagogical best practices, we've created a learning environment that bridges the gap between traditional textile education and modern industry requirements.

Our platform enables students to explore textile science through hands-on, interactive experiences that were previously impossible in traditional classroom settings. Instead of memorizing fabric characteristics from textbooks, students can now analyze real fabric samples instantly, understand consumption calculations through practical examples, and develop industry-relevant digital skills that prepare them for modern fashion careers.

Integrated Learning Modules

Our structured curriculum guides students through progressive learning stages:

Module 1: Introduction to AI in Fashion

Understanding Fabric Identification Technology

Learn how AI and machine learning work, understand CNN models, and explore the basics of digital textile analysis

Module 2: Textile Science Fundamentals

Fiber Types and Fabric Structures

Study natural and synthetic fibers, weave patterns, knit structures, and how to identify them using FabricSense

Module 3: Practical Identification Skills

Hands-On Fabric Analysis

Practice identifying 50+ fabric types, interpret AI confidence scores, and validate results with physical samples

Module 4: Consumption Calculation

Master Fabric Estimation Methods

Learn professional consumption formulas, understand marker efficiency, and plan garment production budgets

Module 5: Sustainable Design Practices

Minimizing Waste and Environmental Impact

Apply consumption estimation to reduce fabric waste, explore sustainable materials, and design responsibly

Module 6: Industry Applications

Real-World Project Work

Complete capstone projects simulating professional scenarios in design studios and manufacturing environments
