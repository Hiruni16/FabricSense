Building a Greener Fashion Future

The fashion industry is one of the world's largest polluters, producing 92 million tons of textile waste annually. FabricSense is pioneering a solution by providing AI-powered fabric consumption prediction that helps designers, manufacturers, and students reduce waste at the source.

Our technology empowers conscious design decisions that minimize environmental impact while maximizing creative potential. By accurately predicting fabric requirements and optimizing cutting layouts, we help the fashion industry take meaningful steps toward sustainability.

Carbon Footprint Reduction

Decreased fabric production means lower energy consumption and fewer greenhouse gas emissions from manufacturing and transportation.

Circular Economy Support
