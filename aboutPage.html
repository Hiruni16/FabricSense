About FabricSense

Revolutionizing fabric analysis and consumption estimation through the power of artificial intelligence

What is FabricSense?

FabricSense is an innovative AI-based system designed to revolutionize the way fashion students, designers, and textile professionals identify fabrics and estimate consumption requirements. By leveraging cutting-edge machine learning technologies, we provide accurate, data-driven insights that enhance learning, improve efficiency, and promote sustainable practices in the fashion industry.

Our platform combines the power of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for fabric identification with advanced regression models for consumption estimation, all wrapped in an intuitive web interface that makes sophisticated AI technology accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

To empower fashion students and professionals with AI-driven tools that simplify fabric identification and consumption estimation, bridging the gap between traditional textile education and modern technological innovation.

Our Vision

To become the leading platform for AI-assisted fabric analysis in fashion education, promoting sustainable design practices and digital literacy in the textile industry worldwide.

Why FabricSense?

Traditional fabric identification and consumption estimation methods rely heavily on manual observation and experience-based calculations, which are time-consuming, subjective, and prone to errors. FabricSense addresses these challenges by:

  • Providing instant, accurate fabric identification from uploaded images
  • Offering data-driven consumption estimates based on garment specifications
  • Reducing material waste through precise calculations
  • Enhancing learning experiences for fashion students
  • Bridging the gap between academic knowledge and industry practices
  • Promoting sustainable and eco-friendly design approaches
